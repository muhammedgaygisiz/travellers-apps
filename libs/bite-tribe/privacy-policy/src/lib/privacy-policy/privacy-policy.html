Privacy Policy for BiteTribe

Last updated: February 2026

BiteTribe (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use the BiteTribe mobile application and related services (the “App”).

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you sign in to BiteTribe, we collect:

  • Your authentication identifier (e.g. user ID)
  • Your email address (if you sign in with email)
  • Your display name and profile image (if provided by Google or Apple sign-in)

Authentication is handled securely via Firebase Authentication.

1.2 User-Generated Content

When you use the App, you create and share content, including:

  • Photos of meals (“Bites”)
  • Restaurant or place names
  • Prices
  • Ratings
  • Location data associated with a Bite

This content is stored to provide the core functionality of the App.

1.3 Location Data

Location data is used only when you create or view Bites to:

  • Attach a location to a Bite
  • Show nearby food posts

Location access is optional and controlled by your device permissions.

1.4 Usage Data

We may collect basic usage data to understand how the App is used and to improve it, such as:

  • App interactions
  • Crash reports
  • Performance data

This data is collected using Firebase services and does not personally identify you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and operate the BiteTribe App
  • Allow users to create, view, and share Bites
  • Authenticate users and secure accounts
  • Improve app performance, stability, and user experience
  • Communicate important updates related to the App

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored using Google Firebase services.
  • Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect your information.
  • No system can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we take security seriously.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

Your data may be shared only in the following cases:

  • Public content you choose to share (e.g. Bites) is visible to other users
  • With service providers (such as Firebase) strictly to operate the App
  • If required by law

5. Third-Party Services

BiteTribe uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Firestore
  • Firebase Storage
  • Firebase Analytics (if enabled)

These services are provided by Google and are subject to Google’s privacy policies.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access your personal data, request correction or deletion of your data, and withdraw consent where applicable.

You can delete your account and associated data by contacting us.

7. Children’s Privacy

BiteTribe is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
