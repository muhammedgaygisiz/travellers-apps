- [epic: moderate review threads with edit, delete and reporting](https://github.com/muhammedgaygisiz/travellers-apps/issues/1284) (Issue \#1284)
- Description
  - \# Epic: moderate review threads with edit, delete and reporting
  - Split out of \#1283, which adds threaded replies to Bite reviews but deliberately stops short of letting anyone change, remove or report what has been written.
  - \#\# Why this is separate
  - \#1283 answers one question: how does a conversation happen under a Bite. It does not answer what happens when that conversation goes wrong, and the two need different decisions.
  - A review today cannot be edited or deleted at all. `ReviewApiService` exposes `reviewsByBiteId` and `saveNewReview` and nothing else, so an author who made a typo or regrets a comment has no path.
  - Once \#1283 lands, a review is no longer a leaf. Deleting a root review that carries replies destroys other people's contributions, so deletion needs a rule - tombstone, cascade, or refusal - before it can be built.
  - [[Bite]] already records admin moderation as an operational capability that is not modelled as a Bite permission. Reporting needs that gap closed rather than worked around.
  - Review text is the first place in BiteTribe where one user writes something another user is notified about. \#1283 widens that from one notification to a whole thread, which raises the ceiling on abuse before any moderation exists.
  - \#\# Goal
  - An author can correct or withdraw what they wrote, a reader can report content they should not have to live with, and an operator can act on those reports - without a thread losing the contributions of people who are not at fault.
  - \#\# Scope
  - Edit own review or reply, with an edited marker and `updatedAt` maintenance
  - Delete own review or reply, including the rule for a root review that has replies
  - A per-item report action with a reason
  - A report store and an operator surface, most plausibly on the existing business `migrations` page rather than a new app
  - Operator actions: hide content, keep it, and whatever account-level consequence is decided
  - Firestore rules covering who may write what, which cannot stay "every authenticated user may write every document" for this
  - Localized copy for every new affordance, in all supported locales
  - Storybook states for edited, deleted and hidden items
  - \#\# Success criteria
  - An author can edit their own review or reply and no one else's, proven by a rule deny test.
  - Deleting a root review with replies follows a recorded decision and never silently removes another user's reply.
  - A reported item reaches an operator surface where it can be acted on, and the reporter is not exposed to the reported author.
  - A hidden item disappears from the thread without renumbering or breaking the replies around it.
  - Notification fan-out from \#1283 skips deleted and hidden items rather than notifying about content that is no longer visible.
  - \#\# Out of scope
  - Threaded replies themselves - \#1283 owns those.
  - Automated or model-assisted content classification.
  - Moderation of any surface other than review threads.
  - \#\# Product decisions to confirm
  - Deleting a root review that has replies: tombstone the root and keep the thread readable, cascade-delete the whole thread, or refuse the delete while replies exist? Proposal: tombstone, because the replies belong to other people.
  - Is an edit window time-limited, or can a review be edited forever? Proposal: unlimited but marked, since a silent edit after a reply is the actual problem, not a late one.
  - Who is an operator? There is no admin role in the model today, so this needs one.
  - Does a report notify anyone, or is it silent until an operator acts? Proposal: silent.
  - Does a hidden item stay visible to its own author?
  - \#\# Validation
  - Focused Nx targets for each touched project, Functions tests for any callable, and Firestore emulator rule tests with at least one allow and one deny case per new rule.
