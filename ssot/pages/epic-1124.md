- Description
  - \# Epic: BiteTribe Pro subscriptions
  - Part of \#1121 (stage 2).
  - Let a food lover pay for BiteTribe Pro and receive a trustworthy entitlement, so discovery beyond their immediate surroundings becomes a product they can buy.
  - \#\# Why this exists
  - The free tier is bounded by physical presence: today's 15 km radius around the user. The moment a user plans a trip, wants to search a city they are not standing in, or wants to find a specific dish rather than browse what is nearby, they hit the boundary. That boundary is the product's natural price line, and it maps exactly onto the backend capabilities that cost money to run.
  - \#\# Rails decision
  - Subscriptions run on Apple and Google in-app purchase through RevenueCat. Apple and Google require their own billing for digital subscriptions, so there is no realistic alternative on native. RevenueCat is used rather than raw store plugins because it provides server-side receipt validation, renewal and grace-period handling, and webhooks into Cloud Functions, which is the majority of the work in a self-built integration. Its fee is accepted as the price of not owning that correctness problem.
  - The PWA is out of scope. It stays free until a separate decision is taken.
  - \#\# Depends on
  - Stage 0. This epic writes into the entitlement store built there and must not introduce a second source of truth.
  - \#\# Scope
  - Pricing, packaging and store product configuration in App Store Connect and Play Console
  - RevenueCat integration in the app
  - A RevenueCat webhook that writes the server-owned entitlement
  - Purchase, restore and manage-subscription flows
  - Subscription lifecycle: renewal, billing retry, grace period, cancellation, refund, expiry
  - Subscription state visible in settings and on the profile
  - Store-required legal copy and auto-renew disclosure
  - Conversion and churn analytics
  - Purchase and restore test coverage
  - \#\# Success criteria
  - A user can buy Pro on Android and iOS and gains Pro capabilities without restarting the app.
  - Restoring purchases on a reinstall or a second device returns the entitlement.
  - A lapsed, refunded or cancelled subscription removes Pro capability through the webhook, not through a client check.
  - Cancelling is reachable from inside the app, as the stores require.
  - The purchase flow passes App Store and Play review, including the auto-renew disclosure requirements.
  - \#\# Out of scope
  - A creator or business tier.
  - Web or Stripe billing.
  - Family sharing, gifting and promotional codes beyond what the stores offer natively.
  - Win-back and churn campaigns. Those follow once churn data exists.
  - \#\# Product decisions to confirm
  - Price point and billing periods. Proposal: a monthly price plus a discounted annual price, since annual materially improves cash flow and retention at this scale.
  - Free trial or introductory offer? Proposal: a 7 day trial on the annual plan only.
  - Does Pro carry over to the business app? Proposal: no, they are separate products.
  - \#\# Validation
  - Functions tests for the webhook including signature verification and replay, Nx targets for the touched libraries, Storybook states for the paywall and subscription settings, and a sandbox purchase plus restore on a real Android and a real iOS device.
