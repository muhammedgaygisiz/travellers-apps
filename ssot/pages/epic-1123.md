- [epic: AdMob advertising for free users](https://github.com/muhammedgaygisiz/travellers-apps/issues/1123) (Issue \#1123)
- Description
  - \# Epic: AdMob advertising for free users
  - Part of \#1121 (stage 1).
  - Earn revenue from free users through Google AdMob without damaging the dish-first discovery loop, and without shipping an ad request before the user has legally consented to it.
  - \#\# Why this stage can run early
  - Ads are the only channel that needs no purchase rails, no store products and no payout infrastructure. It is the fastest path from zero to non-zero revenue. Only the "hide ads for Pro" child depends on stage 0, so the rest can proceed in parallel.
  - \#\# Current state
  - There is no AdMob dependency in `package.json` and no ad code anywhere in the workspace.
  - There is no consent management platform in the app. \#989 covers GDPR consent mode for analytics and overlaps this work; the two consent surfaces should be designed together rather than as two separate prompts.
  - \#542 already describes the intended first placement and is re-scoped as the native in-feed child of this epic.
  - \#\# Goal
  - A free user sees tasteful native ads inside the home feed, at a frequency that does not break the browsing rhythm, after a compliant consent flow. A Pro user sees none.
  - \#\# Scope
  - Ad policy: formats, placements, frequency caps, and what content must never sit next to an ad
  - AdMob SDK integration for Android and iOS through Capacitor, with a clean no-op on web
  - Google User Messaging Platform consent flow plus the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt
  - Native in-feed ad units in the home feed
  - Suppressing every ad placement for Pro users
  - Ad performance analytics
  - A deferred spec for the remaining formats
  - \#\# Compliance constraints
  - No ad request may be made before the UMP consent flow has resolved for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. This is a hard gate, not a nice-to-have.
  - On iOS, personalized ads additionally require the App Tracking Transparency prompt. A user who declines must still get non-personalized ads rather than a broken feed.
  - The App Store and Play Console listings must declare advertising and the data it collects. The privacy manifest and data-safety form both need updating.
  - AdMob has no web SDK. The PWA build must not attempt to load ads and must not show empty ad slots.
  - \#\# Success criteria
  - A free user on Android and iOS sees a native ad card in the home feed at the agreed frequency, and it is visually distinguishable as an ad.
  - No ad request is fired before consent resolves, verified in network logs.
  - A Pro user sees zero ad placements and zero ad requests.
  - The web build renders the feed with no ad slots and no console errors.
  - Ad revenue, impressions and click-through are reportable next to the existing analytics.
  - \#\# Out of scope
  - Interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads in this first slice. They are specified but not implemented, pending in-feed acceptance data.
  - House ads or promoting BiteTribe's own paid BiteTrails as ad inventory.
  - Any ad network other than AdMob.
  - \#\# Product decisions to confirm
  - What frequency? \#542 proposes every 5 bites. Proposal: start at every 8th card and tune from retention data, because 5 is aggressive for a feed whose cards are the product.
  - Should ads appear anywhere other than the home feed in this slice? Proposal: no.
  - Do we run a single global consent prompt covering analytics and ads, or two? Proposal: one, designed with \#989.
  - \#\# Validation
  - Focused Nx targets for the touched libraries, Storybook states for the feed with and without an ad card, a device check on Android and iOS for the consent flow, and a web build check proving no ad code runs.
