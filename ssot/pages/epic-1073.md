- Description
  - \# Epic: Table payment and Bite creation from orders
  - Part of \#735 (stage 4).
  - Close the loop: the guest pays, the visit ends, and the dishes they actually ordered become the easiest Bites they will ever create.
  - \#\# Goal
  - BiteTribe already knows the restaurant, the dish, the price, the currency, and the menu context of an order. Creating a Bite from it should require only a photo, a rating, and a comment.
  - This is where the restaurant interaction platform pays back into the core product, and it is the original intent of \#735 (menu items linked to Bites).
  - \#\# Scope
  - Payment provider and market compliance evaluation, as a decision document before any implementation
  - Pay in the app, or request payment from staff
  - Close the visit and show a receipt or visit summary
  - Select an ordered dish and create a Bite with prefilled restaurant, dish, price, and currency
  - Persist the link between menu item and Bite in both directions
  - Analytics for the order to Bite conversion funnel
  - \#\# Why the evaluation comes first
  - Payment is the only part of \#735 that carries regulatory and financial risk rather than product risk. Whether BiteTribe touches funds at all changes the architecture, the provider choice, the receipt obligations, and the market rollout order. That decision is made in a spec issue and recorded as an ADR before any payment code is written.
  - The Bite-creation half of this epic does not depend on payment. If payment stalls, prefilled Bite creation can still ship on top of a visit closed by staff.
  - \#\# Success criteria
  - A guest can settle a table bill, or signal that they want to pay, and the table returns to the correct live state afterwards.
  - After a visit closes, the guest sees the dishes they ordered and can create a Bite from any of them.
  - A Bite created this way is prefilled with the verified `restaurantId`, dish name, price, and currency, and needs only photo, rating, and comment.
  - A menu item shows the Bites created from it, and a Bite created from an order references its menu item.
  - The conversion funnel from order to published Bite is measurable.
  - \#\# Product decisions to confirm
  - Does BiteTribe hold or route funds, or is payment always settled by the restaurant? Proposal: restaurant-settled first, BiteTribe never a payment institution.
  - Which market is first, and what receipt and fiscalisation rules apply there?
  - Can a guest create a Bite from an order without a BiteTribe account? Proposal: no, this is the natural account upgrade moment.
  - What happens to the prefilled Bite if the guest never publishes it? Proposal: offer it once at visit close and once in a notification, then drop it.
  - Are prices from an order trusted as Bite prices without validation? Proposal: yes, an ordered price is stronger evidence than a user-typed one, and it should bypass the suspicious-price warning from \#967.
