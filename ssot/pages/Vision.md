- BiteTribe Vision

  We believe food is one of the most authentic ways to experience a place.

  Our vision is to build the world's largest community-driven map of real dishes, allowing anyone, anywhere, to instantly discover what people actually ate—not just which restaurant they visited.

  We want to replace polished marketing with genuine experiences. Every bite shared contributes to a living record of local food culture, helping travelers, locals, and food lovers make better decisions through the experiences of other people.

  In the future, discovering food should feel as natural as discovering places. Whether you're in Tokyo, Istanbul, Zurich, or a small village, you should be able to open BiteTribe and immediately see what people are eating around you, who recommends it, and why.

  Beyond discovery, we envision an ecosystem where passionate food creators can build communities, curate food journeys, and earn money from their local knowledge, while businesses benefit from authentic recommendations instead of traditional advertising.

  Our mission is simple: **Show people what to eat, through the experiences of other people.**
