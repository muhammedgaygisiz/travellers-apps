- Description
  - \# Epic: Restaurant floor plan and table configuration
  - Part of \#735 (stage 1).
  - Restaurant owners can create and maintain a structured two-dimensional floor plan containing rooms, tables, chairs, and operationally relevant objects, and can generate a QR code for every table.
  - \#\# Goal
  - A restaurant owner digitally recreates the dining area well enough that staff and guests recognise it, and that every table becomes an addressable business entity.
  - This is explicitly **not** a construction plan. It is a practical, easy-to-maintain top-down representation.
  - \#\# Scope
  - Create one or more rooms or floors
  - Define room dimensions and boundaries
  - Add walls, doors, counters, bar areas, blocked areas, and decorative objects
  - Place tables (rectangular and round) and chairs
  - Move, resize, rotate, and delete objects
  - Assign table number or name, seating capacity, and enabled state
  - Save, reload, draft, and publish a plan
  - Generate an opaque QR token per table
  - Provide printable QR sheets
  - \#\# Explicitly not in this stage
  - Live table state or occupancy
  - Guest ordering
  - Payments
  - Reservations
  - Any guest-facing surface
  - The floor-plan editor must be useful and shippable without any of them.
  - \#\# Model
  - Follows the shared decisions in \#735:
  - Room-relative integer millimetres, origin top-left, x right, y down. Rotation in degrees clockwise.
  - Rooms hold their dimensions and their non-table geometry objects in one document.
  - Every table is its own document under `/restaurants/{restaurantId}/tables/{tableId}`.
  - Table entity fields:
  - | Field | Meaning |
  - | --- | --- |
  - | `id` | Internal identifier, stable for the table's lifetime |
  - | `label` | Public table number or name shown to staff and guests |
  - | `roomId` | Owning room |
  - | `position` | `{ x, y }` in room millimetres, centre of the table |
  - | `size` | `{ width, height }` in millimetres, or `{ diameter }` for round |
  - | `shape` | `rectangle` or `round` |
  - | `rotation` | Degrees clockwise |
  - | `seats` | Seating capacity |
  - | `enabled` | Whether the table is in service |
  - | `qrTokenId` | Reference to the opaque QR token |
  - \#\# Success criteria
  - An owner can build a two-room plan with at least twenty tables and reload it identically.
  - Table 12 is retrievable as a business entity with its room, capacity, and position, without parsing an image.
  - The same plan renders correctly on desktop, tablet, and mobile from identical stored data.
  - Every enabled table has exactly one active QR token, and tokens are not derivable from the table number.
  - A printed QR sheet is legible and identifies restaurant, room, and table in human-readable text next to the code.
  - Editing the plan never writes to any live-state or ordering collection.
  - \#\# Non-functional targets
  - A plan of 100 objects stays interactive (drag at 60fps on a mid-range tablet).
  - A plan load is one room document plus one tables query, not per-object reads.
  - Concurrent edits from two devices cannot silently overwrite each other.
