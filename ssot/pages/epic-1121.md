- [epic: Monetization (ads, subscriptions, paid BiteTrails)](https://github.com/muhammedgaygisiz/travellers-apps/issues/1121) (Issue \#1121)
- Description
  - \# Epic: Monetization
  - BiteTribe has three revenue channels. This epic is the umbrella that sequences them and holds the shared product decisions so each stage epic stays implementable on its own.
  - \#\# Why now
  - Monetization is Priority P1 and post-launch. Nothing here blocks the 3 to 16 August public launch. It is prepared during Phase 4 of [[Current State - Roadmap]] rather than after it, because the parts with real lead time (store products and review, RevenueCat setup, consent flow, creator payout onboarding) cannot be started on the day the decision to monetize is made.
  - Gating capabilities while the user base is still small would suppress exactly the usage signals Phase 4 exists to collect. So the sequencing below deliberately puts the trustworthy entitlement foundation and the ads channel first, and the two paid channels behind them.
  - \#\# The three channels
  - | Channel | Who pays | Rails | Stage |
  - | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  - | Ads | Advertisers, shown to free users | Google AdMob | Stage 1 |
  - | Subscriptions | Food lovers who want more than their surroundings | Apple and Google in-app purchase through RevenueCat | Stage 2 |
  - | Paid BiteTrails | Food lovers buying a curated journey | Apple and Google in-app purchase through RevenueCat, creator payout through Stripe Connect | Stage 3 |
  - \#\# Product decisions taken
  - Two tiers only: Free and Pro. The profile badge already renders tiers 0, 1 and 2, so a third tier stays possible later without a model change.
  - The free tier is exactly today's product: the 15 km radius around the user's current position loaded through `loadBitesByLocation`, with client-side search and filtering inside that result set.
  - Pro unlocks backend search (`searchBites`, `searchBitesByCity`, `searchRestaurants`, `searchUsers`), loading Bites at a position or city other than the current one, a radius beyond 15 km, and an ad-free app.
  - Content creation stays free. Supply is what makes discovery worth paying for.
  - Map zoom stays free for everyone. Issue \#503 proposed gating it and was closed, and [[UC - Discover Bites]] records zoom as tier-independent. It is deliberately not revived.
  - Entitlement is server-owned. A billing webhook writes an entitlement document and an auth custom claim, and every gate is enforced in Cloud Functions and Firestore rules. `subscriptionTier` on the public user document becomes a display-only mirror.
  - Paid BiteTrails sell through store in-app purchase. The creator receives 80 percent and BiteTribe 20 percent of net proceeds, meaning the amount remaining after the store commission. At a 30 percent store cut that is 56 percent of gross to the creator and 14 percent to BiteTribe. Every user-facing, contractual and dashboard wording must say net proceeds and never gross.
  - Ads start with native in-feed ads in the home feed. Interstitial, rewarded and app-open formats are planned but deliberately deferred until in-feed acceptance data exists.
  - \#\# Sequencing
  - Stage 0 is the entitlement foundation. It blocks stages 2 and 3.
  - Stage 1 ads can run in parallel with stage 0. Only its "hide ads for Pro" child depends on stage 0.
  - Stage 2 subscriptions turns the entitlement into recurring revenue.
  - Stage 3 paid BiteTrails depends on stage 0 for entitlement-scoped content access, and on \#1069 for verified organisation identity before any payout can be made to a creator.
  - \#\# Success criteria
  - A free user gets exactly today's 15 km experience plus ads, and every Pro capability is refused by the backend rather than only hidden in the UI.
  - A user can subscribe, restore a purchase and lose access when the subscription lapses, with entitlement state driven by webhooks and not by client reports.
  - A creator can publish a paid BiteTrail, a user can buy it, and the creator can see and receive 80 percent of net proceeds.
  - Revenue and conversion per channel are visible in analytics.
  - \#\# Out of scope
  - A creator or business subscription tier. Business-side monetization stays with \#735.
  - Restaurant transaction fees or commission on table orders. That belongs to \#1073.
  - Web and PWA billing. AdMob has no web SDK and store in-app purchase does not apply on web, so the PWA stays free and ad-free until a separate decision is taken.
  - Regional pricing beyond what App Store Connect and Play Console provide out of the box.
  - \#\# Open product questions
  - What price point and which billing periods? Decided in the stage 2 pricing spec.
  - Is there a free trial or an introductory offer, and does it apply per store or per account?
  - What is the minimum payout threshold and payout schedule for creators?
  - Does a purchased BiteTrail stay accessible after a refund, and what happens to a Bucket List created from it?
  - Does the PWA ever monetize, or does it stay a shop window for the native apps?
