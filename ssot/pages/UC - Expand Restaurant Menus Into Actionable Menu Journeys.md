# UC - Expand Restaurant Menus Into Actionable Menu Journeys

## Status

Next to implement.

## Goal

Menu items should become actionable starting points for food discovery and restaurant interaction.

## Actors

- Food lover
- Traveler
- Restaurant owner or business maintainer

## Target Flow

- User opens a menu item.
- User can create a Bite from the menu item, contact the Restaurant, plan a visit, check availability, or reserve where supported.
