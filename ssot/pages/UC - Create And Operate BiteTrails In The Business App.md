# UC - Create And Operate BiteTrails In The Business App

## Status

Supported today and still expanding.

## Goal

Organisations and creators can create curated BiteTrail offerings.

## Actors

- Organisation admin
- Food curator or vlogger
- Business user

## Current Flow

- Organisation opens dashboard.
- Organisation creates a BiteTrail.
- BiteTrail becomes available as a marketplace-related journey.

## Supported Evidence

- Business `:organisationId/dashboard`
- Business `:organisationId/create-bite-trail`

## Related GitHub Scope

- Issue \#266 includes assigned users, organisation profile handling, BiteTrail creation from selected Bites, free BiteTrail access, and marketplace listing.
