- Description
  - \# Epic: Staff table management and live table state
  - Part of \#735 (stage 2).
  - Turn the saved floor plan into a live operational view that restaurant staff use during service.
  - \#\# Goal
  - Staff open the floor plan and see, at a glance, which tables are free, occupied, reserved, or being cleaned, and can change that state in one interaction.
  - \#\# Scope
  - A live table state model stored separately from the floor plan
  - A defined state machine with allowed transitions
  - Live plan view with colour-coded and text-labelled table status
  - Staff actions: seat guests, free, reserve, mark cleaning, disable
  - Table visits representing a party at a table over time
  - Real-time sync between staff devices and tolerance for poor connectivity
  - Staff accounts with a narrower permission set than owners
  - Analytics on table operations
  - \#\# State machine
  - Proposed states and transitions. Confirm before implementation.
  - `available` to `reserved`, `occupied`, `cleaning`, or `disabled`
  - `reserved` to `occupied`, `available`, or `disabled`
  - `occupied` to `ordering`, `awaitingPayment`, `cleaning`, or `available`
  - `ordering` to `awaitingPayment`, `occupied`, or `cleaning`
  - `awaitingPayment` to `cleaning` or `available`
  - `cleaning` to `available` or `disabled`
  - `disabled` to `available`
  - `ordering` and `awaitingPayment` are reached from stage 3 events. In stage 2 they are reachable only manually, so the model is complete before ordering exists.
  - \#\# Design rules
  - Live state lives at `/restaurants/{restaurantId}/tableStates/{tableId}` and is never written by the floor-plan editor.
  - The floor plan is never written by a state change.
  - A table with no state document is treated as `available`.
  - Every transition records who made it and when, so a disputed table has a history.
  - Transitions are applied through the backend, not by direct client writes, so concurrent staff actions resolve deterministically.
  - \#\# Success criteria
  - Two staff devices viewing the same room see a state change within a second of each other.
  - Two staff members seating a party at the same table at the same moment produce one seating and one clear rejection, never two conflicting states.
  - Deleting or moving a table in the editor does not lose or corrupt its live state.
  - Disabling a table in the editor is reflected in the live view and blocks seating.
  - The staff view remains readable and usable after a connectivity drop, and reconciles without duplicate transitions on reconnect.
  - \#\# Product decisions to confirm
  - Must staff confirm occupancy, or may a guest scan occupy a table automatically? Proposal: staff confirm in the first iteration; a guest scan raises a pending signal rather than occupying.
  - Can guests choose a table themselves? Proposal: no in the first iteration, configurable per restaurant later.
  - When is a table available again? Proposal: after an explicit staff action, optionally after a configurable cleaning timeout.
  - Who can close or reopen a visit? Proposal: any staff member of that restaurant, recorded in the audit trail.
