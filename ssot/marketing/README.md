# Marketing

BiteTribe's social media channels, the posts published on them, and the brand
artwork they use. Not specification: nothing here says how the product behaves.

The artwork is under [assets/](assets/): `characters/` holds the brand characters, which
the `Brand/Characters` Storybook page also renders, and `social/` holds the published
post images, `launch-post.png` among them, written by `npm run generate-launch-post`.

The store listing pages and screenshots are not here. The store review binds them to
[Implementation - Store Listing Assets](../implementation/store-listing-assets.md) and
`ssot/assets/store-listing/`.
