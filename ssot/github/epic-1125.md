- [epic: Paid BiteTrails and creator revenue share](https://github.com/muhammedgaygisiz/travellers-apps/issues/1125) (Issue \#1125)
- Description
  - \# Epic: Paid BiteTrails and creator revenue share
  - Part of \#1121 (stage 3).
  - Turn BiteTrails from a free curation feature into a marketplace where a creator can sell a curated food journey and get paid for it.
  - \#\# Why this exists
  - [[Vision]] promises an ecosystem where food creators can earn money from their local knowledge, and [[Market Place]] already carries `price`, `currency` and sell records for BiteTrails. What is missing is everything between a price field and a creator receiving money: a purchase, an entitlement on the purchased content, a ledger and a payout.
  - \#\# Current state
  - A BiteTrail has `price` and `currency` and is free when `price` is 0 ([[Bite Trail]]).
  - Saving a BiteTrail as a Bucket List writes a lightweight sell record under `/biteTrails/{biteTrailId}/sells`, which is how `soldCount` is derived. There is no payment behind it.
  - Nothing prevents a user from reading a priced BiteTrail's Bites today.
  - \#\# Revenue model
  - Paid BiteTrails sell through store in-app purchase, the same rails as stage 2. A curated journey delivered as in-app content is digital content, and selling it outside store billing is an app-review risk that is not worth taking for the first iteration.
  - The creator receives 80 percent and BiteTribe 20 percent of net proceeds, where net proceeds means the amount remaining after the store commission. At a 30 percent store commission that is 56 percent of gross to the creator and 14 percent to BiteTribe.
  - Gross-based wording is forbidden everywhere, because 80 percent of gross plus a 30 percent store cut exceeds the sale price and cannot be honoured. Terms, the creator dashboard, the publish flow and any marketing copy must all say net proceeds.
  - Payouts run through Stripe Connect Express, so identity verification, tax details and payout scheduling stay with Stripe.
  - \#\# Depends on
  - Stage 0, for entitlement-scoped access to purchased content.
  - Stage 2, for the RevenueCat integration this reuses.
  - \#1069, for verified owner identity. Money must not be paid to an unverified claimant.
  - \#\# Scope
  - The commerce model and the revenue-share terms
  - BiteTrail pricing, publication and purchase state on the model
  - Purchase through store in-app purchase
  - Entitlement-scoped access to a purchased BiteTrail's content, with a locked preview before purchase
  - Marketplace listing, preview and purchase UI
  - Creator pricing and publish flow in the business app
  - A creator earnings ledger implementing the 80/20 net split
  - Stripe Connect payout onboarding and payouts
  - A creator earnings and payout dashboard
  - Conversion and earnings analytics
  - \#\# Success criteria
  - A creator can price and publish a BiteTrail, and cannot publish a paid one before payout onboarding is complete.
  - A user can buy a paid BiteTrail and immediately access its Bites, and a user who has not bought it cannot read them, proven by an emulator rule deny test.
  - Every sale produces a ledger entry whose creator and platform amounts reconcile against the store payout report.
  - A creator can see pending and paid earnings and receives a payout.
  - A refund reverses the ledger entry and revokes access.
  - \#\# Out of scope
  - Subscription bundles that include paid BiteTrails.
  - Creator-set discounts, campaigns and coupon codes.
  - Selling BiteTrails as bookable real-world experiences outside store billing. That is a separate compliance decision and would need \#1109 first.
  - Multi-currency creator payouts beyond what Stripe Connect supports natively.
  - \#\# Product decisions to confirm
  - Are price points free-form or restricted to store price tiers? Proposal: store price tiers, because in-app purchase requires configured products anyway.
  - What does a non-buyer see? Proposal: name, image, location, description, Bite count and a blurred or partial Bite list.
  - Minimum payout threshold and schedule? Proposal: monthly, with a threshold matching Stripe's practical minimum.
  - Does a purchased BiteTrail stay accessible forever, and what happens to a Bucket List created from it after a refund?
  - Who is liable for VAT on the sale? Under store in-app purchase the store handles it in most markets, and this must be confirmed per market before launch.
  - \#\# Validation
  - Functions tests for the ledger and purchase verification, emulator rule tests for purchased and unpurchased access, Nx targets for the touched libraries and the business app, Storybook states for locked and unlocked marketplace cards, and a sandbox purchase on a real device.
